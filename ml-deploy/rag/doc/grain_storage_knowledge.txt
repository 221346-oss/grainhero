GRAINHERO GRAIN STORAGE OPERATIONAL MANUAL
==========================================

TEMPERATURE THRESHOLDS BY GRAIN TYPE
--------------------------------------
Wheat: Safe storage below 15C. Above 25C triggers rapid spoilage. Ideal: 10-15C.
Maize (Corn): Safe below 18C. Above 28C accelerates mycotoxin development. Ideal: 12-18C.
Rice: Safe below 20C. Above 30C causes quality loss. Ideal: 15-20C.
Sorghum: Safe below 20C. Above 28C increases aflatoxin risk. Ideal: 12-18C.
Barley: Safe below 15C. Above 22C triggers hotspot formation. Ideal: 10-15C.

MOISTURE CONTENT SAFE LIMITS
------------------------------
Wheat: Safe below 13.5%. Critical above 15%. Above 16% = immediate spoilage risk.
Maize: Safe below 13%. Critical above 14.5%. Above 15.5% = high mold risk.
Rice: Safe below 14%. Critical above 15.5%. Above 17% = rapid deterioration.
Sorghum: Safe below 12.5%. Critical above 14%.
Barley: Safe below 13%. Critical above 14.5%.

HUMIDITY CONTROL
-----------------
Relative humidity in storage should be kept below 65%.
Above 70% RH: mold growth accelerates on grain surface.
Above 75% RH: insect activity increases significantly.
Condensation risk occurs when temperature differential exceeds 5C between grain mass and ambient air.
Aeration fans should activate when RH outside is lower than inside the silo.

CO2 MONITORING
---------------
CO2 below 600 ppm: Normal grain respiration, safe condition.
CO2 600-1000 ppm: Elevated respiration, monitor closely, check moisture.
CO2 1000-1500 ppm: Warning - active biological activity, inspect for hotspots.
CO2 above 1500 ppm: Critical - immediate inspection required, potential spoilage in progress.
CO2 above 3000 ppm: Emergency - evacuate personnel, do not enter silo without ventilation.

VOC (VOLATILE ORGANIC COMPOUNDS)
----------------------------------
VOC below 200 ppb: Normal background level.
VOC 200-500 ppb: Mild off-gassing, possible early mold presence.
VOC above 500 ppb: Significant microbial or pest activity detected.
VOC above 1000 ppb: High probability of active spoilage or pest infestation.

AERATION PROTOCOLS
-------------------
1. Cooling aeration: Run fans when outside air is 5C or more cooler than grain.
2. Drying aeration: Run continuously when grain moisture exceeds safe limit by 1%.
3. Equilibrium aeration: Run when outside RH is below 65% to equalize grain moisture.
4. Aeration fan runtime: Minimum 4 hours per session for effective temperature equalization.
5. Never aerate during rain or when outside RH exceeds 85%.
6. Recommended aeration: 2-4 CFM per bushel for cooling.

PEST DETECTION AND MANAGEMENT
-------------------------------
Common stored grain pests: Weevils, Grain Borers, Flour Beetles, Grain Moths.
Early signs: Fine dust or frass on grain surface, grain clumping, unusual warmth in localized areas.
Temperature hotspots (more than 5C above average) often indicate pest or mold activity.
VOC spikes alongside temperature hotspots: 90% probability of active infestation.
Action threshold: 1-2 insects per kg of grain - immediate fumigation required.
Preventive treatment: Apply approved protectant when loading new grain batches.

HOTSPOT DETECTION
------------------
A hotspot is defined as any point in the grain mass where temperature exceeds the average by 3C or more.
Hotspots form due to moisture migration, biological activity (mold or insects), or structural issues.
Response to hotspot:
  Level 1 (3-5C above average): Increase monitoring frequency to every 6 hours.
  Level 2 (5-10C above average): Activate aeration immediately, inspect visually.
  Level 3 (more than 10C above average): Emergency - consider transfer or fumigation.

NATURAL STORAGE LIFE ESTIMATES
--------------------------------
Wheat at 12% moisture, 15C: 3-5 years
Wheat at 14% moisture, 20C: 6-12 months
Maize at 13% moisture, 18C: 2-3 years
Maize at 15% moisture, 25C: 3-6 months
Rice at 14% moisture, 20C: 1-2 years
Sorghum at 12% moisture, 18C: 2-4 years
Barley at 13% moisture, 15C: 2-3 years

RISK SCORE INTERPRETATION
--------------------------
0-24% (Low): Grain is in optimal condition. Continue routine monitoring every 12-24 hours.
25-49% (Moderate): Conditions slightly outside optimal range. Increase monitoring to every 6 hours. Check moisture and temperature trends.
50-74% (High): Significant risk present. Immediate corrective action required. Activate aeration, inspect physically, consider transfer.
75-100% (Critical): Emergency condition. Grain at immediate risk of spoilage or safety hazard. Take emergency action - transfer, fumigate, or dispose if unsafe.

SENSOR MAINTENANCE
-------------------
Temperature cables: Calibrate every 6 months. Replace sensors showing drift more than 1C vs reference.
Humidity sensors: Clean monthly. Replace annually or when reading variance exceeds 3% RH.
CO2 sensors: Zero-calibrate every 3 months using fresh outside air reference.
VOC sensors: Replace sensing element annually for accuracy.
Regular maintenance reduces false alarms by 60% and improves prediction accuracy.

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
---------------------
Fire in silo: Do not open hatches - this feeds oxygen. Call emergency services immediately.
CO2 above 3000 ppm: Do not enter. Evacuate 20m radius. Ventilate for minimum 30 minutes before entry.
Grain avalanche risk: Never walk on grain surface. Use approved entry equipment and buddy system.
Structural failure warning: Unusual sounds, cracks in walls, or uneven settling - evacuate immediately.

SILO MANAGEMENT BEST PRACTICES
--------------------------------
Always inspect grain before loading - reject visibly moldy, wet, or insect-damaged grain.
Clean silos thoroughly between batches to remove old grain, dust, and debris.
Sample grain at multiple depths and locations - top, middle, and bottom of the grain mass.
Maintain a minimum 1 meter headspace above grain for airflow and inspection access.
Record all temperature and moisture readings with timestamps for trend analysis.
First In First Out (FIFO): Always use oldest grain first to prevent long-term storage issues.
Rotate grain every 6-12 months even if conditions are good, to prevent caking and stratification.

GRAIN QUALITY GRADES
---------------------
Grade 1 (Premium): Moisture below 12.5%, no visible damage, no foreign material.
Grade 2 (Standard): Moisture 12.5-14%, minimal damage (less than 2%), minimal foreign material.
Grade 3 (Feed): Moisture 14-16%, visible damage (2-5%), requires immediate attention.
Below Grade: Moisture above 16%, significant damage (more than 5%), not suitable for human consumption.

LOADING AND UNLOADING SAFETY
------------------------------
Never enter a silo during loading or unloading operations.
Minimum 2-person team required for any silo entry.
Lockout/tagout all mechanical equipment before entry.
Test atmosphere (CO2, O2) before entry after extended storage.
Wear appropriate PPE: dust mask minimum, respirator for fumigated grain.
